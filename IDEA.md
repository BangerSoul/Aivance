AI Career Copilot is an all-in-one intelligent job search and career management platform that automates the entire employment journey. The platform aggregates job opportunities from multiple job portals worldwide, helps users apply automatically, tracks application statuses, and manages follow-ups with recruiters.

Key features include:

🌍 Global Job Scraping & Discovery from multiple job boards and company career pages.
🤖 One-Click AI Job Applications with personalized resumes and cover letters.
📊 Application Tracking & Follow-Up Management with reminders and status updates.
📄 AI Resume Builder & Editor featuring multiple ATS-friendly templates.
✍️ Smart Cover Letter Generation tailored to specific roles and companies.
📧 HR Contact Discovery to identify recruiter emails and hiring manager contacts for targeted outreach.
🎯 AI Interview Preparation with mock interviews, role-specific questions, and feedback.
🧠 Integrated AI Assistant available throughout the platform to guide users at every step.
💻 Local & Open-Source AI Model Support enabling privacy-focused, cost-effective AI interactions without heavy cloud dependence.

Vision:
 To become a personal AI-powered career agent that discovers opportunities, optimizes applications, connects candidates with recruiters, and prepares them for interviews, significantly increasing job search efficiency and success rates.
